Shisen-Sho

Big Tile Shisen-Sho is a clean and readable Mahjong Connect puzzle game by minsunsoft. Match identical tiles using classic Shisen-Sho rules. Tiles can be connected when the path uses no more than two turns.

Why Big Tile Shisen-Sho?

Large and Clear Tiles

Bigger tiles and readable symbols help players scan the board quickly on a phone screen.

Classic Shisen-Sho Gameplay

Find matching pairs and clear the board using the familiar Mahjong Connect rule set.

Multiple Difficulty Levels

Play relaxed puzzles or more challenging stages depending on how much pressure you want.
